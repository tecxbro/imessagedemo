`settle` waits for the committed revision, fonts, images, the explicit scroll checkpoint, screen-space bubble bottoms, and the arrival or bubble-effect pose. The frozen `RendererHandle` type does not list `settle`; see `docs/change-requests/WT-03.md`.

## What the adapter does

`IosMessagesApp` is mounted at `iosScreen` (402×874). Demo messages become pinned `Message` values with numeric `sentAt`. Typing stays on `typing`. Link, attachment, image, and audio payloads are copied without download `href`s. The status clock is UTC from `nowMs` (the foundation instant is `4:41`). Light and dark come from a `.dark` ancestor around the shell.

Text and emoji arrivals use `sendAnimation` / `receiveAnimation` with explicit progress. At the source duration the cue is omitted so the send ghost and clone are removed. Slam, loud, and gentle use `useBubbleEffectOnMessage` and do not also run the send flight. Invisible-ink drift and typing dots keep the source CSS animations and are paused at a cue-relative delay. Scroll is written to the end of the log after layout, with `overflow-anchor: none`, including when a reverse seek does not change the message count.

Playback sets `pointer-events: none` on the device and cancels link navigation. `IosInspectSession` keeps its buttons outside the device and remounts on reset.

Catalogue scenes use the pinned components. FaceTime and system lines are standalone. Editable bubble, undo-send, controlled audio, selection, and swipe times are standalone because the shell does not expose those controls. Long press, thread, effects picker, plus menu, details, photo picker, image viewer, and screen effect go through the shell overlay or its overlay props. Image viewer source rects are converted with `viewportRectToFrame`. The shell is not scaled.

## Source limitations

- The pinned `Message` has `reactions`, `replyTo`, `replyCount`, `edited`, and `readAt`. `DemoMessage` does not. Tapback, reply, and edited are catalogue scenes, not timeline frames.
- `RenderFrame` has no scroll offset and no overlay state. The checkpoint scroll is the end of the transcript. Overlays are catalogue-only.
- `screenEffectDuration` and `iosScreenTransition` are `unverified` in `motion-tokens.json`. FaceTime has no capture in the pin. System lines are not a `MessageKind`.
- A seeked send animation never finishes, so the pin leaves the ghost and clone mounted at progress 1. The adapter drops the cue at the endpoint instead of patching that.
- The shell measures long-press rects in viewport pixels. The device is kept at 1:1 CSS pixels. `viewportRectToFrame` corrects an outer scale for scripted image-viewer rects.
- Polls, Photon mini apps, group details, and the unpinned `imessage.swerdlow.dev` modules are out of scope.

## Integration

A player can render `<IosDemoRenderer ref={ref} compiled={compiled} frame={frame} />` and call `seek` plus `element` through `RendererHandle`. `settle` is on the same object and needs the owned `IosRendererHandle` cast until the contract change lands. Catalogue scenes do not depend on the compiler, runtime, or player lanes. `projectFrame` reads frozen `CompiledDemo` events directly.
