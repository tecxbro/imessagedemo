# WT-04 handoff

Optional scene directives, owned by this lane, seek conversation switches, menus, arrivals, bubble effects, screen effects, and reply threads without editing the pin or the contracts.

## Behavior

- The window is `macScreen` (960×640, sidebar 330) in light and dark. Traffic lights, sidebar, header, bottom-anchored log, and composer come from `MacMessagesApp`.
- `DemoMessage` maps onto the pinned `Message` fields the schema already has. Sender, edited, reactions, reply snapshots, and reply counts are copied when a fixture carries them.
- Sidebar previews are derived from the last authored message. Transcript text is not rewritten. Unread dots and iCloud footers stay off unless a fixture sets them.
- A mid-switch frame mounts the departing conversation, then applies the target and seeks `conversationTransition`. Fresh, sequential, backward, and repeated captures of the same checkpoint matched in Chromium.
- Context menus take pane coordinates and the message id. Reply threads use `ReplyThread` as `overlay`. `thread` is not passed to `MacMessagesApp`. There is no iOS long-press overlay.
- One arrival owner: `sendAnimation` / `receiveAnimation` on the shell. Progress at or above 1 releases the flight so the settled frame has no send clone or ghost. A reply is not flown, because the shell's selector hits the stub bubble first.
- `whenReady` waits for the committed revision, fonts, decoded images, bottom scroll, and screen-space `--bubble-bottom` fills. A stale revision or an iOS screen rejects. `reset()` drops inspect metadata.

## Source limitations

- `macTransitions` and `macWindowMetrics` are unverified in `motion-tokens.json`. Screen-effect timings are unverified.
- `FaceTimeCard`, `SystemMessage`, `EditedLabel`, and `UndoSendPoof` are catalogue visuals. They are not transcript kinds.
- `useArrivalAnimation` uses the first `[data-slot="message-bubble"]` in the row. That node is the reply stub when a message quotes another. The adapter skips that flight.
- Polls, Photon mini apps, and provider unread or iCloud state are absent.
- iOS list, new-message, and effects-picker scenes are rejected.

## Integration readiness

WT-00 can mount `MacDemoRenderer` with a macOS `RenderFrame` today. A single conversation, draft, and typing flag need no scene. Switches, menus, arrivals, effects, and threads need `MacSceneInput` until the contract grows. Do not point the player at this branch's compiler, runtime, or iOS renderer; those stubs still throw.
