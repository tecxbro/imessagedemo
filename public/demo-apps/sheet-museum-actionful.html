Choose an Object

Three objects and the details that make them extraordinary.

The terracotta vase

Clay becomes a vessel through pressure, water and heat. Follow the carved leaves around its warm surface. This object is an invented illustration for our local demo.

The bronze mirror

A circular face catches the light. The green patina gives this imagined bronze piece its quiet depth; turn your attention to the birds and branches.

The cobalt bottle

Deep blue glass becomes almost black in shadow and electric at the rim. Notice how the rounded body gathers and bends the gallery light.
